# E-Commerce Prompt Templates

Production-ready prompt patterns for common e-commerce image needs.

## Alibaba Detail Image (阿里详情图)

### White background product shot
```
Professional product photography of {product}, placed on a clean white marble
surface with soft natural lighting from the left, subtle reflection underneath,
minimalist composition with negative space on the right for text overlay,
ultra-detailed, commercial e-commerce style, 4K quality, shot on 85mm lens, f/2.8
```
Size: 2K, Ratio: 1:1 or 4:3

### Lifestyle scene
```
{product} being used in a cozy modern living room, warm afternoon sunlight
through large windows, bokeh background, lifestyle product photography,
aspirational mood, high-end e-commerce detail page style
```
Size: 2K, Ratio: 16:9

## Product Poster (产品海报)

### Hero shot
```
Hero product shot of {product} centered on a gradient {brand-color} background,
dramatic studio lighting with rim light, water splash effect around the product,
bold and eye-catching composition, premium poster design style, ultra-sharp detail
```
Size: 2K, Ratio: 9:16 (mobile) or 1:1 (square)

## Website Hero Image (官网图)
```
Wide cinematic shot of {product} on a modern wooden desk in a bright minimalist
office, large window with city view in background, soft diffused lighting,
clean aesthetic with ample negative space for headline text, premium tech brand
```
Size: 2K, Ratio: 16:9

## Image-to-Image: Background Swap
```
Place this product on a clean studio background with soft gradient lighting,
maintain product details and proportions, add subtle shadow underneath for
realistic grounding, e-commerce white background style
```
Mode: img2img

## Video: Product Demo (产品演示视频)
```
Cinematic close-up of {product} slowly rotating on a pedestal, studio lighting
with soft gradients, shallow depth of field, premium product demonstration,
smooth camera movement, commercial quality
```
Mode: text2video, num_frames: 81 (3s), frame_rate: 24
